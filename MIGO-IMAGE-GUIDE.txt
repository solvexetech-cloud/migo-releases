💡 BEST PRACTICES
─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Use JPG for photos (smaller size)
2. Use PNG for logos (transparent)
3. Always check on mobile after upload
4. Avoid images with text (unreadable on mobile)
5. Test different lighting conditions
6. Food photos: bright, colorful, warm tones
7. Business cover: horizontal, wide, inviting
8. Square logo for best circular display

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
─────────────────────────────────────────────
❌ Portrait images for cover (cut off)
❌ Dark or blurry photos
❌ Watermarked images
❌ Text-heavy images
❌ Wrong aspect ratio
❌ Over 10 MB (Cloudinary rejects)
❌ Wrong format (e.g., GIF, BMP, TIFF)

🎯 HOW AUTO-RESIZING WORKS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
When you pick an image from gallery/camera:
  1. App reads original image
  2. Auto-resizes to max dimensions (800 or 1600)
  3. Auto-compresses to 85% quality
  4. Uploads to Cloudinary
  5. Cloudinary stores as-is (no further compression)
  6. Returns secure URL (https://...)

Result: Fast upload, reasonable file size, good quality

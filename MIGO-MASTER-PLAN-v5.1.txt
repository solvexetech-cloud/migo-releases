## ഭാഗം 3: Database Schema (Updated)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### New Collections (Community Demand)
- `demands/{id}` — userId, userName, title, description, targetPrice, currency, requiredCount, currentCount, status (open/threshold_reached/locked), createdAt, expiresAt, areaId
- `demand_joins/{id}` — demandId, userId, userName, joinedAt
- `demand_bids/{id}` — demandId, businessId, businessName, offerPrice, authenticity (Original/Master Copy), fulfillmentType (Store Pickup/Home Delivery), status (pending/approved/rejected), businessTrustScore, adminNotes, createdAt
- `demand_claims/{id}` — demandId, bidId, userId, businessId, businessName, finalPrice, fulfillmentType, deliveryAddress, deliveryPhone, redemptionPin, qrData, status (locked/redeemed/cancelled), createdAt, expiresAt, redeemedAt

### Existing Collections (Updated)
- `notifications/{id}` — userUid, title, body, type, data, read, createdAt

📌 1. STANDARD DEAL (സ്റ്റാൻഡേർഡ് ഡീൽ)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
MIGO Code:   OfferType.standard
Label:       "Standard Deal"
Duration:    1-30 days (recommended: 7)
Discount:    10-30% OFF
Best For:    Everyday discounts

When to use:
  ✅ Regular combo meals
  ✅ Menu items with steady discount
  ✅ Normal products
  ✅ Everyday services

Example:
  Title: "Family Shawarma Meal"
  Original: QAR 40 | Offer: QAR 30
  Save: QAR 10 (25% OFF)
  Duration: 7 days

Business Benefit:
  → Steady customer flow
  → Predictable revenue

👥 3. GROUP DEAL (ഗ്രൂപ്പ് ഡീൽ)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
MIGO Code:   OfferType.group
Label:       "Group Deal"
Duration:    3-14 days
Discount:    20-40% OFF
Best For:    Volume discounts
Special:     Min participants needed

When to use:
  ✅ Family meals (4+ people)
  ✅ Party packages
  ✅ Bulk orders
  ✅ Group activities
  ✅ Business lunches

Example:
  Title: "Family Pizza Night"
  Original: QAR 80 | Offer: QAR 55
  Save: QAR 25 (31% OFF)
  Min people: 5
  Max people: 20 (optional)
  Duration: 7 days

Business Benefit:
  → Bigger orders
  → Word-of-mouth marketing
  → Group bookings

How it works:
  1. Customer joins group
  2. When min participants reached → "UNLOCKED"
  3. All participants get the deal
  4. Auto-notification on unlock

Visual: "GROUP DEAL" badge
Extra fields: Min people, Max people

🎯 OFFER CREATION CHECKLIST
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before creating any offer:
  ✅ Title — Clear, 3-5 words, appealing
  ✅ Description — 1-2 lines, key benefit
  ✅ Original Price — Real market price
  ✅ Offer Price — Actual deal price (must be < original)
  ✅ Offer Type — Choose from 5 (see above)
  ✅ Duration — Right time window (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days)
  ✅ Image — 16:9, bright, food close-up
  ✅ Daily Limit — Optional (per-day cap)
  ✅ Terms — Any conditions (optional)
  ✅ Group Min — Only for Group Deal type

💰 PRICING STRATEGY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Price Ranges in MIGO:
  Low:     QAR 1-20   → Coffee, snacks, small items
  Medium:  QAR 20-100 → Meals, services
  High:    QAR 100+   → Premium, packages

Recommended Discounts:
  Standard:   15-25%
  Flash:      30-50%
  Group:      20-35%
  Hidden Gem: 40-60%
  Birthday:   Free or 50%

Formula for Savings:
  Savings = Original - Offer
  Percent = (Savings / Original) × 100

Example:
  Original: QAR 40
  Offer: QAR 30
  Savings: QAR 10
  Percent: (10/40) × 100 = 25% OFF

⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
─────────────────────────────────────────────
❌ Fake discounts (original price inflated)
❌ Offer price = Original price (must be less)
❌ Unrealistic durations (1 hour)
❌ No image (fewer clicks: 3x less claims)
❌ Bad image (dark, blurry)
❌ Too many offers at once (dilutes focus)
❌ Complex terms
❌ Group min < 2 (invalid)
❌ Wrong type for the offer

🔔 WHAT HAPPENS AFTER CREATION?
─────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Offer status: "pending" (auto)
2. Admin reviews via admin panel
3. Admin approves → status "live"
4. Customer sees on Home → Flash/Nearby
5. Customer claims → +5 coins
6. Business redeems QR → +25 coins
7. Offer expires automatically at endAt

Notifications:
  • Deal expiring soon (24h before)
  • Group deal unlocked (for group deals)
  • New review (for business owner)
